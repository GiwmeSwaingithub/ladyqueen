B. Data Collected Automatically

C. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or any identifying information

  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Journal content or any text you write
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID)

8. Data Security

DJOURNAL implements the following security measures:

  • All journal data is stored using Android's standard local storage, protected by your device's security layer (PIN, fingerprint, etc.)
  • No network connections are made by the core application

  • The developer strongly recommends enabling device-level encryption and regular device backups via Google Drive or other backup services
Important – Data Loss Risk Because all data is stored locally on your device, Kemboi254 cannot recover, restore, or access any journal entries under any circumstances. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all entries. Please maintain regular device backups.

About DJOURNAL

Application overview and feature description

DJOURNAL is a lightweight, offline-first journaling application for Android designed to encourage daily reflection. The core philosophy: three sentences a day is enough to preserve memories, appreciate moments, and build a lasting record of your life.

📱

Offline First

Works with zero internet connection. Your writing habit doesn't depend on a server.

🔒

Zero Data Collection

No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics on your content. Your words stay on your device.

✍️

Daily Habit

Write 3 sentences about your day. Small input, big impact on memory and gratitude.

⚡

Instant Start

No sign-up flow. Open the app and start writing immediately after installation.
